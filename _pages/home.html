Welcome to the University of Miami's Geodynamics Group. We are located within the Department of Marine Geoscience at the Rosenstiel School of Marine, Atmospheric, and Earth Science, and led by Prof. Adam F. Holt.

Research

We investigate the causes, rates, and scales of deformation at plate boundaries, within plates, and in the underlying mantle. We integrate geodynamic modeling techniques with geological and geophysical observations. Some recent topics include:

  1. Regional subduction dynamics. Analytical and numerical models linking mantle flow and pressure to near-surface deformation and tectonics.
  2. Subduction zone thermal structure, metamorphism, and dehydration. Numerical models of feedbacks between slab geometry, convergence, thermal structure, and phase transformations.
  3. Plate tectonic analyses. Mechanical rules for present-day tectonic observations and tests of plate-reconstruction dynamic feasibility.
  4. Global subduction and mantle dynamics. Analytical and global numerical models of slab-induced mantle flow and slab-dip distributions.

Teaching

  • Natural Disasters: Hollywood vs. Reality (GSC 107): Introductory science course on geologic hazards and societal response. Syllabus
  • Geodynamics (MGS 724): Graduate course on rheology, heat and mass transport, and numerical methods. Lecture notes and exercises
  • Subduction Zone Geodynamics (MGS 710): Literature-based overview of slab deformation, mantle flow, rheology, and subduction-induced topography. Syllabus, Schedule
