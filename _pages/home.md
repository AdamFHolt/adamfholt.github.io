​​​​​​​**Welcome to the webpage of the University of Miami's Geodynamics Group, located within the [Rosenstiel School of Marine and Atmospheric Sciences](https://www.rsmas.miami.edu) and led by [Adam F. Holt](https://people.miami.edu/profile/aholt@rsmas.miami.edu).**
<br>
<br>
We strive to isolate the forces that govern the geologic evolution of plates, plate boundaries and the underlying mantle, and, in turn, constrain the rates and scales over which deformation occurs. To achieve this, we integrate a variety of geodynamic modeling techniques with geological observations. See the Research page for a sampling of current/recent projects.
