<h3 style="font-weight: bold">Teaching</h3> 

<hr>
<p style="padding-top:5px">
**Geodynamics (MGS 724)**: This is a graduate-level course covering the physical properties of the solid Earth and the dynamic processes that operate within it. The main topics covered are deformation and rheology (elasticity, plasticity, and viscous flow), heat and mass transport, and numerical methods (finite differences, finite elements). I have deposited the most recent version of the lecture notes and class exercises [online](https://github.com/AdamFHolt/MGS723_Geodynamics). Feel free to use these as a study resource or as part of your own Geodynamics class.
</p>
<hr>
<p style="padding-top:5px">
**Natural Disasters: Holywood vs. Reality (GSC 107):** This is an introductory science course covering the geological causes and effects of natural hazards on Earth, and the responses of society to them. Lectures introduce the underlying science, and, in a term paper and class exercises, students use these scientific principles to assess disaster films and media articles for their scientific veracity. Here is an example [syllabus](/documents/misc/Syllabus_GSC107.pdf). If you are an educator and would like to access the complete class materials, feel free to send me an email request.
</p>
